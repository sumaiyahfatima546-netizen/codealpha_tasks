TASK 3 – DATA VISUALIZATION
CODEALPHA DATA ANALYTICS INTERNSHIP

INSIGHTS

1. Category Sales:
Electronics has the highest total sales among all product categories.

2. City Orders:
Ahmedabad has the highest number of orders among the cities shown in the visualization.

3. Customer Age:
Customers are distributed across a wide range of age groups, showing participation from both younger and older customers.

4. Price vs Quantity:
There is no clear relationship between product price and quantity ordered. Different quantities are purchased across a wide range of product prices.

5. Customer Rating:
Customer ratings are distributed across approximately 2.5 to 5.0. Ratings around 3 and 3.5 have relatively high numbers of customers.

6. Monthly Sales:
Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year. December 2023 has the highest sales, while April 2023 has the lowest sales.

7. Correlation Analysis:
Product Price has a strong positive correlation with Total Sales (0.87). Quantity has a moderate positive correlation with Total Sales (0.31), while Customer Age and Rating have very weak correlations with Total Sales.
